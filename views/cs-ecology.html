Philosophy

This project explores the idea that computer science, architecture, and ecology are not separate disciplines, but expressions of the same underlying principles.

Algorithms optimize resources. Architecture organizes matter. Ecosystems balance energy. All three are systems shaped by constraints.

Bamboo is used here not as a metaphor, but as evidence — a material where efficiency, adaptability, and sustainability converge.
